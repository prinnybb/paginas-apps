Frequently asked questions

How do I invite my siblings?

Open the Family tab and tap Invite with a link. Send it by WhatsApp, text or email. They tap the link, install Elmora if needed, sign in, and join. The message also includes the code in case they need to type it.

I didn't get my sign-in code.

Check your spam folder and wait a minute before requesting another code.

Reminders aren't showing up.

On iPhone, go to Settings → Notifications → Elmora and turn on Allow Notifications. On Android, go to Settings → Apps → Elmora → Notifications and turn them on; also allow “Alarms & reminders” so reminders arrive on time. Each family member's phone gets its own reminders.

How does "days left" work?

Count the pills when you pick up a prescription and enter the number in the medication. The app subtracts each scheduled dose and reminds you before they run out. When you refill, update the number.

How do I cancel Premium?

On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Elmora → Cancel. On Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Elmora → Cancel. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Family → Delete my account. See the Privacy Policy for what happens to shared family information.

Ayuda en español

Escríbenos a carecircleappai@gmail.com. Para invitar a tu familia: pestaña Familia → Invitar con un enlace, y mándalo por WhatsApp, mensaje o correo. Para cancelar Premium: en iPhone, Ajustes → tu nombre → Suscripciones; en Android, Google Play → Pagos y suscripciones → Suscripciones. Si no llegan los recordatorios, activa las notificaciones de Elmora en los ajustes del teléfono (en Android, también «Alarmas y recordatorios»). Para borrar tu cuenta: Familia → Borrar mi cuenta. En una emergencia médica, llama al 911.
