Frequently asked questions

How do I invite my siblings?

Open Today → Family → Share code and send the code by text message. They download CareCircle, sign in, and enter the code.

I didn't get my sign-in code.

Check your spam folder and wait a minute before requesting another code.

Reminders aren't showing up.

Go to iPhone Settings → Notifications → CareCircle and turn on Allow Notifications. Each family member's iPhone gets its own reminders.

How does "days left" work?

Count the pills when you pick up a prescription and enter the number in the medication. The app subtracts each scheduled dose and reminds you before they run out. When you refill, update the number.

How do I cancel Premium?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → CareCircle → Cancel. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Today → Account → Delete my account. See the Privacy Policy for what happens to shared family information.
