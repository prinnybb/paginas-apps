Care for Mom or Dad, together — without the chaos.

CareCircle keeps the whole family on the same page: medication reminders, who gave which dose, doctor visits, shared expenses, an emergency card for the ER, and AI summaries of what the doctor said.

  • Medication reminders for every caregiver, with refill alerts before pills run out.
  • "Given by Sarah at 8:05 AM" — no more "did Mom take her pill?" texts.
  • Emergency card with allergies, meds and contacts, plus a one-tap 911 button.
  • AI visit notes: dictate what the doctor said; get a clear summary for everyone.
  • Shared expenses: copays, Medicare premiums, rides — clear accounts between siblings.
