Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026 · Applies to the CareCircle iPhone app
In short: CareCircle stores the care information your family enters so that everyone in your family circle can see it. We don't sell your data, we don't show ads, and we don't track you across other apps. You can delete your account at any time from inside the app.

Who we are

CareCircle is operated by LCM Capstone Co. ("we", "us"). Questions: carecircleappai@gmail.com.

Information we collect

WhatExamplesWhy
Account informationEmail address, the name you choose to show your familyTo sign you in and show family members who did what
Care information you enterMedications, doses and schedules, doses given, appointments, expenses, notes about doctor visits, emergency card (allergies, conditions, contacts, insurance)To provide the app's features to you and your family circle
Subscription statusWhether your family has Premium (we never see your card details; Apple handles payment)To unlock Premium features
Basic technical dataError logs, app versionTo keep the app working and secure

Care information may include health information about the person you care for. Please only enter what your family needs to coordinate care.

How we use it

  • To run the app: sync information within your family circle, send reminders, and create PDF reports you request.
  • AI summaries: when you tap "Summarize with AI", the text of that note is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to create the summary. It is used only to produce the summary and is not used to train AI models.
  • To provide support and keep the service secure.

We do not sell your information, use it for advertising, or track you across other apps and websites.

Who can see your information

  • Your family circle: only people you invite with your circle's code can see that circle's information.
  • Service providers that run parts of the app for us, under contracts that limit how they use data: Supabase (secure database and sign-in), Anthropic (AI summaries), Apple and RevenueCat (subscriptions), and our email provider (sign-in codes).
  • If required by law, or to protect someone's safety.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit, and database rules make sure each family can only access its own circle. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your information, we will notify you as required by law, including the FTC Health Breach Notification Rule.

Keeping and deleting your information

  • Delete your account anytime: Today → Account → Delete my account.
  • If you are the only member of a family circle, the whole circle and all its information is deleted.
  • If other family members remain, the shared care records stay for them, but they are no longer linked to your name or email.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription; cancel it in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Consumer health data (Washington, Nevada and similar laws)

Some U.S. states protect "consumer health data". We collect it only to provide the features you use, share it only as described above, and never sell it. You may request access to, or deletion of, your data at carecircleappai@gmail.com, or delete it yourself in the app.

Your privacy rights (including California)

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to know, access, correct or delete your personal information, and to not be discriminated against for using these rights. We do not sell or "share" personal information for advertising. To make a request, email carecircleappai@gmail.com.

Children

CareCircle is for adults who coordinate care. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

HIPAA

CareCircle is a tool for families, not a healthcare provider or insurer, so it is generally not covered by HIPAA. We still protect your information as described in this policy.

Changes

If we make important changes, we will tell you in the app before they take effect.
